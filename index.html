// BEFORE WE BEGIN

A conversation lens, not a claim.

This tool was built to help the audience think through whether the current doctrinal operational framework — Deep / Close / Rear — is sufficient to describe corps operations in a 2040 operating environment. Please read the notes below before we proceed.

  • Purpose. The tool exists to support a deliberate conversation about the adequacy of our current operational framework for 2040, not to advocate for a specific answer.
  • No assertion of insufficiency. I am not claiming that the current doctrinal framework is insufficient. That is the question under discussion, and it is the audience’s to weigh.
  • No claim of subject-matter expertise. I am not presenting as a doctrinal authority. This is a prototype built to structure a discussion, not to render a professional judgment.
  • EXP 2040 is exploratory. The alternative framework presented here was assembled from concepts, doctrine-related reading, and recurring themes and talking points I have observed from senior leaders and general officers. It is exploratory — not an approved construct and not doctrine.
  • Support discussion, not conclusion. Any conclusions drawn today belong to this audience. The graphics are a scaffolding for dialogue; they are not a final doctrinal judgment.

Scenario data is notional. The tool runs locally in this browser with no telemetry and no external services.
